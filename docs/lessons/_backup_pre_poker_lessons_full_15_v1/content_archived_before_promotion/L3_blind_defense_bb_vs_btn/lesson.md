# L3 Blind Defense Basics (BB vs BTN)

## Intent
Reduce high-frequency BB overfold leakage versus BTN opens.

## Common leak
- Overfolding BB versus small BTN opens due to position discomfort.

## Why it costs money
- BB defense is one of the most frequent 6-max preflop nodes.
- Even small defend-frequency leaks can burn a significant share of total winrate.

## Learning outcomes
- Apply defend thresholds by price + realization context.
- Convert blind defense into measurable winrate retention.

## 10k-hand tracking target
- Track BB defend % vs BTN open by size bucket (2.0x / 2.2x / 2.5x).
