# L1 Open-Raise Discipline By Position

## Intent
Fix early-position over-open leaks and improve preflop EV retention.

## Common leak
- Over-opening marginal offsuit broadways in EP/LJ despite poor realization.

## Why it costs money
- In tough, rake-heavy pools, these opens are often low-edge or near breakeven.
- Small preflop mistakes in high-frequency nodes compound into meaningful monthly drag.

## Learning outcomes
- Choose profitable EP open/fold decisions by hand class and pool context.
- Reduce reverse-implied EV losses in high-rake/tough reg environments.

## 10k-hand tracking target
- Track RFI by seat (UTG/LJ/CO/BTN) and confirm EP frequency tightening.
